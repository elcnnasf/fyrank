## Why each page earns its place

| Page | Job in the path | What breaks without it |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | States the claim in one line and proves it's real enough to keep reading. | She never learns what I do; she bounces in 4 seconds. |
| `/work/<slug>` ×3 | Carries the entire burden of belief. Before/after visibility on a named site. | The claim stays a claim. Nothing here is checkable, so nothing is believed. |
| `/about` | Answers "who is this person and why does he care?" — the trust gate before she gives me her site. | She believes the work but not the person; she doesn't hand over her URL. |
| `/audit` | The one action. Removes every reason not to do it: free, no call, 3 days, one form. | The belief has nowhere to go. |

## Pages I am deliberately NOT building

| Rejected page | Why it doesn't earn its place |
|---|---|
| `/blog` | A content treadmill that competes with the case studies for the same attention. If I have something to say, it becomes a case study. |
| `/services` + `/pricing` | She isn't buying a package yet; she's deciding whether I'm real. Pricing before proof kills the audit request. |
| `/contact` | Duplicates `/audit` and gives her a weaker second option. One action means one door. |
| `/work` (index) | With three cases, an index page is a click that adds zero belief. The three cards live on the home page. |
| `/resume` or CV download | Wrong artifact for this person. She's not hiring an employee; she wants her problem looked at. |
| `/testimonials` | Testimonials belong inside the case study they came from, next to the numbers they're vouching for. |
| `/faq` | Every real FAQ answer belongs on `/audit`, where the objection actually fires. |

## Navigation
